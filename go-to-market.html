The Real Challenge

Most GTM efforts start too late.

"Go-to-market strategy is the complete architecture for how a company will acquire, expand, and retain its ideal customers — spanning positioning, pricing, sales motion, marketing amplification, and customer success playbooks."

Most companies begin thinking about GTM after they've built their product. Smart ones start thinking about it before they start building — understanding the market, the buyer, the willingness to pay, and the path to sustainable competitive advantage. That's where real GTM strategy lives. SOM Strategies helps you build it — whether you're 12 months from launch or already in market and looking to scale.

Our Approach

Building a GTM strategy that scales.

Three integrated phases designed to give you competitive clarity and a repeatable engine for customer acquisition.

Phase 01

Market Intelligence & Positioning

We start by building a complete picture of your market — the competitive landscape, buyer personas, willingness to pay, alternative solutions, and where your product has defensible advantage. From that foundation, we develop a positioning strategy that resonates with your highest-value buyer and differentiates you from realistic alternatives.

  • Competitive positioning and differentiation analysis
  • Ideal customer profile (ICP) definition and validation
  • Buyer journey mapping and decision criteria
  • Pricing research and willingness-to-pay assessment
  • Messaging and value proposition refinement
Phase 02

Value Architecture & Sales Motion

With positioning locked, we design your value architecture — the specific features, benefits, and proof points that matter to your ICP — and the sales motion that will turn that architecture into customer conversations. We cover pricing, packaging, sales playbooks, and the operational infrastructure that scales.

  • Value proposition refinement and proof point development
  • Pricing strategy and packaging architecture
  • Sales process design and qualification framework
  • Sales playbooks and talk tracks for common objections
  • Marketing-to-sales handoff criteria and SLAs
Phase 03

Execution & Scaling

With strategy locked and GTM engine designed, we support the execution phase — ensuring your sales and marketing teams are aligned, your lead qualification process is crisp, your win rates are tracked and improving, and your customer success playbooks are setting customers up to become advocates. We measure and optimize relentlessly.

  • Sales team training and readiness certification
  • Marketing campaign calendar and lead generation strategy
  • Pipeline management and forecast accuracy systems
  • Win/loss analysis and objection handling optimization
  • Customer success playbooks and expansion strategy
  • Monthly GTM metrics review and optimization

Our Process

How every engagement runs.

STEP 01

Discovery & Competitive Context

We start with detailed discovery calls with your CEO, sales leader, and product lead. We also conduct competitive research — understanding your realistic alternatives, competitor positioning, and market structure before we engage with your team.

STEP 02

Customer & Win/Loss Interviews

For engagements focused on existing products in market, we conduct structured interviews with your customers, lost deals, and competitive win/loss pairs. These conversations often surface truths your own team hasn't heard.

STEP 03

Strategy Development Workshop

We synthesize research into an interactive strategy workshop with your full leadership team — covering positioning, ICP definition, pricing, and sales motion. Decisions are made in the room. Ownership is assigned on the spot.

STEP 04

GTM Roadmap & Enablement

We develop a detailed GTM roadmap with phases, milestones, and dependencies. We also support sales enablement — training your team on messaging, positioning, and new sales playbooks. Your team understands not just what to say, but why.

STEP 05

Launch & Early-Stage Optimization

We support your launch — whether that's a new go-to-market motion or full market entry. We monitor early sales activity closely, capture win/loss data, and adjust tactics in real time. Speed of learning is your competitive advantage.

STEP 06

Quarterly Reviews & Evolution

We don't disappear at launch. For ongoing advisory engagements, we meet quarterly to review metrics, competitive changes, and market shifts — evolving your strategy as new information becomes available.
