About

About the Practice

State of Mind Strategies is a senior-led commercial growth consulting practice. We help mid-market and enterprise leadership teams turn strategy into measurable revenue, margin, and enterprise value.

Founded by Zachary Leifer

Senior Operators. Measurable Results.

State of Mind Strategies was founded by Zachary Leifer, a commercial growth executive with 15+ years of experience leading marketing and technology at Fortune 500 companies and high-growth platforms. That operating background shapes how the practice works. We don't hand you a deck and walk away. We build alongside your team and stay accountable to the numbers.

The practice draws on a network of senior operators across commercial strategy, AI, and technology, so every engagement is staffed by people who have actually held the role, owned the budget, and lived with the outcome.

How We Work

We don't sell frameworks. We solve problems, build systems, and hold ourselves accountable to the same commercial outcomes our clients are measured on. Every engagement starts with an honest assessment of where you are today: what's working, what isn't, and where the highest-value opportunities sit.

Who We Serve

We work with mid-market and enterprise leadership teams across gaming, hospitality, entertainment, sports, and technology. Most of our clients already know what needs to change. What they need is senior help to prioritize, execute, and prove the result.

Core Expertise

Commercial Growth

P&L management, revenue strategy, pricing & yield management, go-to-market, customer acquisition & retention, and enterprise value creation.

AI & Digital Transformation

AI strategy & governance, digital transformation programs, customer data platforms, personalization architecture, and technology operating models.

Marketing Technology

MarTech stack design, CRM & CDP implementation, marketing automation, performance marketing, analytics infrastructure, and agency governance.

Leadership & Org Design
