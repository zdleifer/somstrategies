Fractional CMO Services

Senior marketing leadership embedded in your organization, strategy, team direction, P&L ownership, and commercial results. Fortune 500 CMO experience on a flexible engagement model.

A fractional CMO is an experienced chief marketing officer who works with your company on a part-time or retainer basis, providing the same strategic leadership, team direction, and commercial accountability as a full-time CMO, at a fraction of the cost. Fractional CMOs are ideal for mid-market companies that need senior marketing leadership without the commitment or expense of a full-time executive hire.

What We Do as Your Fractional CMO

We embed a senior marketing leader into your team to run the function as a true executive, not an outside consultant who submits decks and disappears. We build strategy, manage your team, own the budget, drive accountability, and report results to your CEO and board.

Our leadership spans CMO of a $1.5B revenue organization, VP of Digital Marketing at The Venetian Las Vegas, and CCO of a global technology platform. We've managed marketing P&L, built teams from scratch, secured capital investment at the board level, and delivered sustained revenue growth through data-driven commercial discipline.

If your marketing isn't performing at the level your business needs, or you're scaling and don't have the right leadership in place, a fractional CMO engagement can accelerate your results immediately.

What's Included

  • Marketing strategy and annual planning
  • Team structure, hiring, and performance management
  • P&L ownership and budget management
  • Agency selection, management, and accountability
  • Go-to-market strategy and execution
  • Customer acquisition and retention strategy
  • Brand strategy and positioning

Why Fractional

The Case for a Fractional CMO

A full-time CMO costs $250k–$400k per year in total compensation, before benefits, equity, or severance. A fractional CMO delivers equivalent strategic leadership at significantly lower cost, with more flexibility.

Fraction of the Cost

Get Fortune 500 CMO experience for $8k–$15k/month, versus $300k+/year for a full-time hire. Plus no benefits, equity, or severance obligations.

Immediate Impact

No onboarding ramp. We've run marketing organizations at scale. We assess quickly, prioritize ruthlessly, and build momentum from week one.

Flexible Engagement

Scale up or down based on your needs. Ideal for companies in transition, going through a growth phase, or filling a gap before a permanent hire.
