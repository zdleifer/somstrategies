The Real Challenge

Why most AI initiatives stall.

"The barrier to enterprise AI value isn't the technology — it's the absence of a coherent strategy, the lack of data readiness, misaligned governance, and leadership teams that haven't yet internalized what AI-native decision-making actually requires."

Most organizations are running AI pilots. Few are capturing AI value at scale. The difference isn't talent or budget — it's strategic architecture. SOM Strategies helps executive teams build that architecture: the strategy, governance, data foundation, and organizational capability needed to move from experimentation to enterprise impact.

Our Process

How every engagement runs.

STEP 01

Discovery Call

A candid 30–45 minute conversation about where you are with AI today — what's been tried, what's worked, what hasn't, and what leadership is expecting. No pitch, no agenda other than understanding your situation clearly.

STEP 02

Landscape & Diagnostic Review

For substantive engagements, we conduct an initial review of your current AI initiatives, data infrastructure, technology stack, and organizational structure. We identify quick wins alongside systemic gaps before scoping any formal engagement.

STEP 03

Engagement Design

We propose a tailored engagement scope — matching the right model (assessment, strategy, or implementation partnership) to your organization's stage, urgency, and investment appetite. All success criteria are defined in writing before work begins.

STEP 04

Executive Alignment Session

Every AI engagement begins by aligning your leadership team — not just the CTO, but the CFO, COO, and key business unit heads. AI strategy fails when it's treated as an IT program. We make sure everyone is reading the same map from day one.

STEP 05

Strategy Delivery & Prioritization

We deliver the strategy in a working session — not by email. Recommendations are stress-tested with your team in real time. Priorities are locked. Owners are assigned. The path forward is clear and agreed upon before anyone leaves the room.

STEP 06

Implementation Oversight & Course Correction

We stay engaged through execution — providing governance, tracking value realization, and flagging drift before it becomes failure. When the AI landscape shifts (and it will), we help you adapt without losing momentum.
